# Research Questions

## Main Research Question

How can Machine Learning be integrated into a smart warehouse management system to improve product demand forecasting, inventory monitoring, and restocking decision support?

## Sub Research Questions

RQ1: How accurately can Machine Learning models forecast product demand compared with traditional forecasting approaches?

Sub-questions

RQ1.1: Can Machine Learning models predict future product demand from historical sales, inventory transactions, seasonal patterns, and promotion-related data?

RQ1.2: Which forecasting approach performs better for warehouse demand prediction: traditional baselines such as Moving Average/ARIMA or Machine Learning models such as Random Forest/XGBoost?

RQ1.3: Can feature engineering methods such as lag sales, rolling averages, product categories, seasonality, and holiday indicators improve forecasting accuracy?

---

RQ2: How effectively can the proposed system recommend suitable restocking quantities based on predicted demand and current inventory status?

Sub-questions

RQ2.1: Can the system calculate restocking recommendations using predicted demand, current stock, lead time, reorder point, and safety stock?

RQ2.2: Can the proposed recommendation method reduce stockout and overstock situations compared with manual or fixed-threshold inventory decisions?

RQ2.3: Can the system provide understandable restocking explanations for warehouse managers?

---

RQ3: How much can the proposed smart warehouse management system improve inventory operation performance compared with traditional spreadsheet-based or manual inventory management?

Sub-questions

RQ3.1: Can the system reduce stockout rate, overstock rate, holding cost, and unnecessary inventory levels?

RQ3.2: Can the system improve inventory turnover, service level, and product availability?

RQ3.3: Can automated low-stock alerts and dashboard-based monitoring help managers make faster restocking decisions?

---

RQ4: How useful and practical is the proposed system for supporting warehouse managers in real-world inventory decision-making?

Sub-questions

RQ4.1: Do warehouse managers or evaluators consider the forecasting results and restocking recommendations useful for daily inventory planning?

RQ4.2: Is the dashboard interface helpful for monitoring current stock, predicted demand, stockout risk, and recommended restock quantity?

RQ4.3: What limitations remain when applying the proposed system to small and medium-sized warehouse or retail environments?
